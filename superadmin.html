Duplicate Flags 0

Raised automatically whenever a business signs up with a name that matches an existing public (Google-sourced) listing on the same island. Removing the public listing hands the page over to the real owner's listing.

Address Mismatches 0

Google-sourced listings whose actual address doesn't mention the island they're filed under — usually a nearby/similar result Google matched to the wrong island during sync. Move each to the right island or remove it.

Renaming an island here updates it everywhere — the picker shown to tourists and business owners, and any existing businesses or events already filed under the old name. Because a few island names repeat across different atolls in the Maldives, the rename is scoped to the atoll you pick it from, but the businesses/events cascade matches on name only.
